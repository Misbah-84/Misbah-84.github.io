Professional Profile & Services

Misbah Ullah

I am Misbah Ullah, a Machine Learning Engineer and Computer Science student at the Institute of Space Technology (IST), Islamabad. I operate at the intersection of statistical analysis, mathematical modeling, and production-grade software engineering, specializing in deploying high-precision computer vision surveillance pipelines, autonomous edge flight algorithms, and clinical decision models.

What We Solved:

  • ConveyorTrack-AI: Engineered a real-time computer vision tracking and sorting system utilizing YOLOv8 and BoT-SORT, establishing extreme counting accuracies for fast-moving manufacturing conveyor belts.
  • Fighter UAV Autonomous System: Built robust edge-AI flight software combining TensorRT-optimized YOLO-OBB targets tracking with pyzbar QR alignment for autonomous fixed-wing UAV landing calculations.
  • VigEye Fight Detection: Developed a multi-tasking deep learning CCTV auditing pipeline to automate real-time threat monitoring, weapon localization, and fire/smoke early alerts.
  • StrokeRisk Decision Ensemble: Crafted clinical prediction engines combining SMOTE-balanced datasets with soft-voting ensemble classifiers (SVM, KNN, Decision Trees) to achieve 94% precision.
